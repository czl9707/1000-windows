## Question

How does a window carry light? Ticket 003 reserves the light genes (on/off, warmth, source) for the insider layer; ticket 013 establishes gene → weighted-table-row colour. Decide: the on/off gene and its street-readable weight (some windows dark at all hours), warmth as a row picked from a weighted OKLCH table with a fantasy threshold above a gene value (the reference's `HAUT_BUNT_AB` pattern), the source gene (lamp versus TV) with TV flicker belonging to motion through `fork(seed, 'tv')`, never the DNA stream, and how light is drawn — a pane wash louder than `GLASS_WASH`, a glow beyond the frame, or both. Then the split the staging question raised: three light-like knobs exist and their boundaries must be explicit — the page theme (the site's light/dark switch, which swaps ink and paper and must re-render every tenant unchanged), the per-window light genes (inside-scene light, independent of the theme), and the global day/night knob from the map's fog (one time-of-day multiplier over the whole wall, deferred until these genes exist). Decide where each knob's authority ends: what a theme switch may never change (gene rolls), what light genes may never change (theme tokens), and whether day/night scales the genes' output or rolls them again. Read ticket 013, the digest's "Colour as roles", and the facade-equivalents table before deciding.
