## Question

Which animals live in the windows? Decide: the pet kinds (cat on the sill, dog at the glass, bird in a cage, fish bowl — few kinds, rolled as kinds), presence weight relative to humans (a pet with no human visible is its own story — allowed or not), the drawing construction per kind (a cat is a silhouette mass with tail; a fish bowl is a measured circle with accessory strokes), how pets share the sill with ticket 015's items (the cat yields space, or the items were never there), pose vocabulary per kind (sitting, curled, at the glass watching the street — named poses, not free angles), and the append-only stream position of the pet block. Read the digest's feature catalogue rules and the beanie/hat body constructions for when a pet earns a real mass (`docs/reference-digest.md`) before deciding.
