## Question

Which lamp constructions exist for the light layer? Build a prototype sheet of ten lamp types seen through the window: home fixtures only — nothing public, street, shop, or industrial (no street lamps, shop signs, office panels). Side silhouette, drawn as flat fills the way the scene would draw them, each carrying a rough sketch of the aura shape it would cast, since the aura's shape follows the lamp. Floor-standing and table lamps stand on the room's floor line; hanging fixtures (pendants and the like) are home fixtures too and may hang from the ceiling line. The user picks which constructions ship in the first pass; the chosen lamps settle the warmth table's hue. Build the sheet at `scratch/lamp-sheet.html` and link it from this ticket.

## Resolution

The first sheet was rejected for leaving the project's language — it predated the colour rule (flat OKLCH washes in one warm family over black night paper) and the ink-line look, and it lit lamp parts that never emit. The redo followed the real drawing language: black paper, white hand-wobbled ink silhouettes, auras as nested flat washes, only the emitting part lit (shade openings, bulb, dish, flame; poles, bases, cords, and stands stay dark).

Picked for the first pass (user, 2026-11-08), sheet positions 1, 3, 4, 5, 7, 8:

1. floor lamp with tapered shade — cone down from the shade's bottom edge, faint band above its top
2. arc floor lamp — round pool hanging under the dome head, mid-air above the floor
3. drum-shade table lamp — bright band at the drum, halos above and below
4. small bedside lamp
5. pendant with shade — wide cone down from the shade
6. bare-bulb pendant — round pool radiating from the bulb itself

Two constructions hang from the ceiling line, four stand on the floor line. The warmth hue follows the sheet's amber family. The round also revised ticket 017's colour model — the aura lays directly over the layers behind it, plus a room-wide ambient fill — recorded as the addendum there. Auras run stronger than the sheet showed.
