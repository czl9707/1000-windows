An infinite hand-drawn wall of windows, built in the open. Every window is a seeded
tenant — its own light, its own curtain, its own small life — drawn with a boiling
line that never repeats between visits. The wall is endless in every direction;
reload and a different wall stands there.

This site documents the construction as it happens: chapters quote the real code
behind each step and record the chain of thought, and the embedded demo is the
engine itself, growing milestone by milestone.
