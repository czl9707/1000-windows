The shell is everything the building owns; the curtain is the tenant's
first possession. It hangs behind the glass — inside the opening, its
points a hair behind the wall plane — so the pane wash draws over it and
a swung casement leaf plausibly swings in front of it. On the wall the
curtain is the first sign that someone lives there: before occupants,
before light, a window that has cloth is a window someone dressed.

## The coverage levels

The stream's first roll decides both whether a window is dressed and how
much cloth it carries, in one pick: **coverage levels** — `0` weight 4,
`0.3` weight 4, `0.6` weight 1, `1` weight 1. The zero level is the bare
window — roughly two in five stand bare, and the glass shows through.
The level is a desired value, not a share; the kind's zone takes it from
there. When the roll lands on zero the stream ends: a bare window rolls
no kind, no jitter, and its tenant DNA simply starts further down —
nothing behind the curtain exists to re-randomize when the vocabulary
grows.

## The kinds

Covered, the roll picks one of four home coverings, weighted toward what
a real street shows: `panels` 4, `drapes` 3, `venetian` 2,
`roman` 2. The catalogue is flat — one roll, one named kind, one
construction — because independent axes multiply into combinations nobody
designed (see [the window shell](/docs/window-shell)). Café tiers,
vertical blinds, sheers, tie-backs, valances: all plausible, all absent.
The append-only rule lets any of them land later as an append — a sheer
is a drapes fill drawn quieter, a tie-back a panels variant, a valance a
trim over any kind — and not one existing tenant re-randomizes.

<DemoCanvas demo="curtain-kinds" label="Demo canvas — the four kinds at a mid coverage" />

**Panels** hang at the sides, one mass per jamb. The coverage measures
the inward reach of each panel as a share of half the opening's width,
so panels always read as dressing, never as covering — their zone
ceiling is 0.4. **Drapes** are one mass hung from a rolled jamb, its
span the coverage's share of the opening's width, ceiling 0.95.
**Venetian** and **roman** hang from the head, and their coverage
measures down as a share of the opening's height — the venetian as
opaque slat rows over transparent gaps (ceiling 0.85), the roman as a
shade whose folds bunch above its bottom bar (0.9).

## Coverage: the zone is the ceiling, not the measure

The rolled level is the desired coverage; the kind's zone is the ceiling
it is clamped to after the draw. The drapes ride the levels nearly
verbatim — 0.3, 0.6, and a full 1 pulled down to their 0.95 — while the
panels pull 0.6 and 1 both onto their dressing ceiling of 0.4: those two
windows draw the same cloth apart from the jitter, because a panel is
dressing, never covering. The gene never pushes a kind out of its
silhouette.

## The cloth

Each construction carries its own cloth role, resolved by styling — the
drawer never names a colour or a strength, the same discipline the glass
wash follows. The cloth resolves in the ink colour at a whisper: a wash
a shade darker than the paper it covers, so the cloth reads against the
wall at any distance yet stays quieter than the lines. The hanging cloth
(panels, drapes) reads as `fabric` and the roman's flat cloth as
`shade`, tuned together at 0.08; the venetian's slat rows read as
`blind` at 0.12 — they are the opaque faces, and what shows between them
is the glass. Every cloth is a base wash, and the fold bands are the
same wash drawn again over it, an irregular number of passes per
band — dark, bare, mid — so the drape darkens where the cloth turns and
mottles between folds without a texture pass. Band boundaries wobble
off their even share on the golden-angle sine, deterministic, no stream
draw. What the pen sees is the hand: hem, fold lines, slats, bars —
every curtain line draws at the texture weight, the catalogue's
lightest, so the cloth's hand reads quieter than the frame's own lines
([the pen](/docs/the-pen) warps each off its trace name). Two verdicts
shape that line work. First, the dense lines obey the hand rule: on
paper nobody draws every line of a dense run — some are omitted
outright, and a kept line runs over only part of its span, the cloth
fill carrying the texture instead (the golden-angle sine picks the
omissions and the extents). Second, the hanging cloth hangs on
verticals alone: the panels and the drapes draw no line across the top,
the fill's edge stating it, and run all the way from head to hem — no
rod clearance above them. The object edges — hems, the roman's bar,
the venetian's rail and the roman's rod — stay full: they are things,
not texture.

## Behind the glass

The cloth's points carry a small negative depth — the curtain stands a
hair behind the wall plane, inside the room — and its marks sort on
their own key between the shell's backing and its glass. That is the
whole reason the layer can sit there: the pane wash draws over the
cloth, the frame draws over the wash, and the projection places the
fabric a touch smaller than the opening it fills ([Motion](/docs/motion)).
No level of detail exists anywhere in the construction: the same marks
draw at wall scale and in a close-up, and the only size variance is the
coverage.

## The rolls

The curtain rolls on the DNA stream, `fork(seed, 'dna')` — the shell
keeps its own fork, so frame vocabulary and tenant vocabulary grow
without re-randomizing each other (the curtain is the tenant's first
layer; the insider genes will append behind it). The order is fixed:
coverage level, then kind, then the measured rolls (rod
jitter ±0.02, width jitter, drape side, blind slat share) — and new rolls
append at the end, never between. A bare window consumes only the
coverage roll:
its stream ends there, and everything appended later lands after it for
every tenant alike. The blind slat share rolls for every dressed
window, mostly light (0 to 0.35, a rare blind reaching 0.8), and
only the venetian reads it: each slat row draws opaque down its rolled
share of the row and transparent below, hard to both jambs, no padding —
the lighter the roll, the more glass shows through the gaps — and the 0
roll is the closed blind, its rows joined into one opaque face.

```ts
export function expandCurtain(seed: number): CurtainPlan {
  const rng = fork(seed, 'dna');
  const level = rng.weighted(COVERAGE_LEVELS);
  if (level === 0) return { present: false };
  const kind = rng.weighted(KINDS);
  const coverage = Math.min(level, ZONE_CEILING[kind]);
  const rodY = rng.float(ROD_JITTER.min, ROD_JITTER.max);
  const widthJitter = rng.float(WIDTH_JITTER.min, WIDTH_JITTER.max);
  const drapeSide = rng.weighted(DRAPE_SIDES);
  const slatShare = rng.outliers(
    SLAT_SHARE_RANGE.min, SLAT_SHARE_RANGE.max, SLAT_SHARE_RANGE.outlierMin, SLAT_SHARE_RANGE.outlierMax,
  );
  return { present: true, kind, coverage, rodY, widthJitter, drapeSide, slatShare };
}
```

`expandCurtain` is the only place that rolls. The drawers are pure
emitters (`panels`, `drapes`, `venetian`, `roman` in
`drawCurtain.ts`): each turns a `CurtainPlan` into
[marks](/docs/the-layers), and `windowSceneMarks(seed, plan)` appends
them behind the shell's — every consumer of `drawWindow` gains tenants
without knowing the layer exists.

## What may change later

Weights may change; kinds may be inserted; sheer, tie-backs, and valance
may append. What may never change is the shape of the promise: a
window's curtain rolls are a pure function of its seed, appended only at
the stream's end. The light genes — a lamp glowing behind the drapes, TV
flicker through them — belong to the light chapter and append there;
this layer hands over the genes that chapter needs, and nothing more.
